# Data permissions and attribution

The repository owner confirmed on 2026-10-30 that permission to redistribute the selected CNEMC monitoring data is available and explicitly authorised publication of these ten-station subsets. This records the owner's confirmation, not an independently retrieved licence instrument. No licence URL or written instrument was supplied to this export; none has been invented.

Monitoring observations originate from the China National Environmental Monitoring Centre national automatic surface-water monitoring publication system. Credit CNEMC and retain provenance when reusing the data. The repository's MIT licence applies to original code and does not purport to relicense third-party monitoring observations. Further downstream redistribution/sublicensing is governed by the applicable source permission; contact the repository owner through GitHub if you require its written terms.

ERA5-Land is a Copernicus Climate Change Service/ECMWF reanalysis product. The ten-station extracts retain source attribution and the dataset's applicable licence; the current official dataset page identifies a CC-BY licence. Dataset DOI: https://doi.org/10.24381/cds.e2161bac . Source and terms: https://cds.climate.copernicus.eu/datasets/reanalysis-era5-land . Scientific reference: Muñoz-Sabater et al. (2021), https://doi.org/10.5194/essd-13-4349-2021 . Processing here uses the frozen point extracts, not a new retrieval.

The physical waterbody descriptors retain their original source URLs and date/water-level conditions in reference/TableS1_waterbodies.csv. They are not synchronous surveys. The map uses Natural Earth public-domain cartography.

Saved predictions and computed benchmark results are generated research outputs. Reuse them with attribution to this tagged benchmark, preserving the distinction between observed and model-generated values.
